What Is Buffer-Free IPTV and Why Does It Matter for Android TV?

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. Instead of using an antenna or satellite, it sends TV streams over your internet connection. For a smooth experience, you need a service that does not pause or freeze — that is “buffer-free.”

SubTV IP uses an anti-freeze server cluster to keep streams stable. It also recommends setting a buffer or cache size of 5,000 milliseconds (ms) in your player. This helps avoid lag on your Android TV box.
